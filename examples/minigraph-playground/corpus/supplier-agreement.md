# Supplier Agreement SA-2026-014

**Parties:** Northwind Office Supplies (the supplier) and Contoso Facilities (the customer)
**Effective:** 2026-01-01 · **Term:** 24 months · **Governing law:** the law of the customer's jurisdiction

## Obligations

- OBL-1: The supplier delivers each purchase order within 10 business days of its confirmation.
- OBL-2: The customer pays each correct invoice within 30 days of its receipt.
- OBL-3: The supplier replaces an item that arrives damaged within 5 business days at no cost to the customer.
- OBL-4: The customer reports a damaged item within 3 business days of delivery.

## Rules

- RULE-1: A late payment accrues interest at 1 percent per month on the amount outstanding.
- RULE-2: Either party may terminate the agreement with 90 days of written notice.
- RULE-3: Prices are fixed for the first 12 months and reviewed once a year after that.

## Deadlines

- 2026-12-31: The first annual price review.
- 2027-12-31: The term ends unless the parties renew it in writing.
