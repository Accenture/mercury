# Service Level Agreement SLA-2026-007

**Parties:** Fabrikam Hosting (the provider) and Contoso Facilities (the customer)
**Effective:** 2026-03-01 · **Term:** 12 months, renewed automatically · **Service:** the facilities booking platform

## Obligations

- OBL-1: The provider keeps the platform available 99.9 percent of each calendar month, maintenance windows excluded.
- OBL-2: The provider answers a severity-1 incident within 15 minutes and resolves it within 4 hours.
- OBL-3: The customer names two technical contacts who may raise incidents.
- OBL-4: The provider publishes a monthly availability report by the fifth business day of the next month.

## Rules

- RULE-1: A month below 99.9 percent availability earns the customer a service credit of 10 percent of that month's fee.
- RULE-2: Service credits are capped at 30 percent of the monthly fee.
- RULE-3: A maintenance window is announced at least 5 business days in advance and falls between 01:00 and 05:00 UTC.

## Deadlines

- 2026-04-07: The first monthly availability report.
- 2027-02-28: The first term ends; the agreement renews unless either party objects 30 days before.
