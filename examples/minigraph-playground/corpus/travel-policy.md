# Travel Policy TP-2026-02

**Parties:** Contoso Facilities (the employer) and its employees
**Effective:** 2026-02-01 · **Owner:** the finance department · **Replaces:** TP-2024-01

## Obligations

- OBL-1: An employee books travel through the approved travel desk at least 7 days before departure.
- OBL-2: An employee files an expense claim within 14 days of the return date, with receipts for every item above 25 euros.
- OBL-3: A manager approves or declines a travel request within 2 business days.

## Rules

- RULE-1: Economy class applies to flights under 6 hours; business class needs the department head's approval.
- RULE-2: Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 euros per night in major cities and 120 euros elsewhere.
- RULE-3: A claim filed after 60 days is not reimbursed.

## Deadlines

- 2026-02-01: The policy takes effect for all new bookings.
- 2026-12-15: The annual review of the reimbursement caps.
