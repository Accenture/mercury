# Data Retention Policy DR-2026-03

**Parties:** Contoso Facilities (the controller) and Fabrikam Hosting (the processor)
**Effective:** 2026-03-01 · **Owner:** the data protection officer · **Scope:** the facilities booking platform

## Obligations

- OBL-1: The processor deletes a booking record 24 months after the booking date.
- OBL-2: The processor deletes an access log 90 days after it is written.
- OBL-3: The controller answers a data subject's access request within 30 days.
- OBL-4: The processor reports a personal data breach to the controller within 24 hours of discovering it.

## Rules

- RULE-1: A record under a legal hold is kept until the hold is lifted, whatever its age.
- RULE-2: A backup copy is deleted within 30 days of the record it holds.
- RULE-3: A deletion is logged with the record's identifier, never with its content.

## Deadlines

- 2026-09-01: The first retention audit.
- 2027-03-01: The annual review of the retention periods.
