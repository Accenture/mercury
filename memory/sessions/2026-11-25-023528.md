  with its model, location and set - replaced whole; `replace_graphs` removes and adds in one swap; a `Refresh` hook
  runs on `get_graph` and `graph_exists` with the id and its location and the lookup re-reads after it;
  `graphs_of_set`, `graph_set`, `graph_location`, `add_deployed_location`, `set_refresh`.
- **`hot_deployment.rs`**, the port of the Java `HotDeployment`: the four keys, `configure` and `configure_with`, the
  per-set record `<id>.deployed.yaml` (six fields, read back through `ConfigReader`), `reconcile_all` after the
  manifests, the reconcile (no record - unregister; record and package disagree - one re-read, then unregister with
  an ERROR; else the strict read, the ownership check, staging in `.staging-*`, the gate, the move, one swap), the
  hook, `deploy`, `undeploy`, `list`, `is_private_subgraph`; `AppError` carries the HTTP status.
- **The REST surface and the switches.** `rest.rs` gains `deploy_graph_set` with the advisory (routes known, unknown or
  remote; extension targets known, unknown or private), `undeploy_graph_set`, `list_deployed_graph_sets`, and the
  OpenAPI entry's 404 for a private subgraph; `lib.rs` the three functions behind `#[optional_service]` on their
  switch; the entries in the test, example and template `rest.yaml`; the keys in the example's and the template's
  `application.yml`, off.
- **Private subgraphs and the provider.** `extension.rs` puts `parent_graph` in the forward; `executor.rs` admits a
  private subgraph from its own set only. `task_server.rs` (nested and flat forms, `/api/event` supplied,
  `event_over_http_with_headers`); `skills.rs` and `suspend.rs` call the provider when the node names one;
  `model_validator.rs::validate_task_server`. `graph_set_loader.rs` shares `gate`, `sha256_hex`, `normalized_path`,
  `folder_of`, `meets_unpack_folder`, and `check_unpack` refuses the hot folder; `compiler.rs` configures before the
  manifests and reconciles after them.
- **One difference the port surfaced:** the state lock is a plain `Mutex`, not reentrant like the Java `ReentrantLock`,

  use the reads that do not, checked by grep.
