**Finding (spec §5 item 5): the edge backstop needs explicit headroom in this
engine.** Java's between-segment edge timeout is the AppStarter housekeeper — a
10-second sweep with a +100 ms buffer — so Java's exact watchdog always acts
first by at least the sweep slack. The Rust edge times its idle allowance
precisely per await, and with both timers at the same value the watchdog LOST
the photo finish (both idle-expiry scenarios rendered the edge's
`Timeout for 2 seconds` instead of the watchdog's outcome). The bridge now
commits the SSE head with `EDGE_GRACE_SECONDS = 10` of headroom — the
housekeeper-interval analog; the watchdog owns idle expiry at exactly
`idle_seconds` and the observable contract is unchanged.
