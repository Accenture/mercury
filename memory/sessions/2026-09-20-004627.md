identical on both: `ProfileCacheL1` tested the reply BODY (`Value::Binary`/`byte[]` or null), never its

have acked `stored`. L2/L3 never had it: the flow and graph engines check task status for the author.

(`redis.cache.enabled=false`), a `#[preload]` stub on `v1.cache.redis` failing fast with 503; L1 GET/POST/
