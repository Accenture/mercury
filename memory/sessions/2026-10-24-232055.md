## Context

Eric asked for the lock-step twin of mercury-composable #591 (squash `ca2344ae`): two simple plugins the field suggested,
`f:addDays(date, days)` and `f:daysBetween(from, to)`, on ISO-8601 `YYYY-MM-DD` dates with no time and no time zone. The Java
rules are the spec: the days are a whole number (an integer, numeric text or a whole float), negative for an earlier date;
`daysBetween` is symmetric (Eric's ruling on the Java side), the earlier date excluded and the later one included; a Nil
argument, a date of another shape, a day the calendar lacks and a result outside 0000 to 9999 are refused by name.

  9999-12-31 (3,652,424) as an `i64`, `num_days().abs()` for the count, every message the Java engine's. `BUILTIN_PLUGIN_COUNT`
