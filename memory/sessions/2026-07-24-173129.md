## Injection-vs-transport diagnosis (the coordinator's open question)

The matrix observation (a Rust callee's envelope view showing the CALLER's
my_route/my_trace_id/my_trace_path, with my_correlation_id and x-event-api
absent) was **(a) transport, not (b) injection**: the Rust delivery path never
injected my_* into the envelope view (only into the copied `headers` param),
and it already removed my_correlation_id + x-event-api from the envelope — so
the three my_* keys that the hello-flow demo copied onto its outgoing envelope
passed straight through as ordinary transported headers.

## What was fixed

with ONE residual: `x-ttl` in the callee's echoed headers when the caller is

changed. New regression `endpoint_timeout_rides_the_dataset_as_the_x_ttl_header`
