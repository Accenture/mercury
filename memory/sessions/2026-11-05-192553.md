## Decision

- **Eric's ruling on the stream's idle allowance: the engine extends the stream.** The Rust HTTP edge times a stream's
  idle allowance exactly, where Java's housekeeper checks every 10 seconds, so a flow's timeout could race the edge's.
  A stream the engine opens declares the flow's TTL plus 5 seconds (`STREAM_IDLE_MARGIN_SECONDS`, one drain plus
  room), so the run's own terminal event always arrives first. A negative control (plain `first`) could not show the
  race in the tests, a few milliseconds wide; the reason is in the code, the skills reference and the Increment.
